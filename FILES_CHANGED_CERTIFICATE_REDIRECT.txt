CERTIFICATE ROUTE REDIRECT - FILES CHANGED
==============================================================
Tanggal: 26 November 2025

PROBLEM:
User mengakses http://localhost:8000/admin/certificates
dan melihat tampilan LAMA (basic upload) tanpa fitur Phase 1-6

ROOT CAUSE:
Ada 2 route berbeda:
- /admin/certificates → CertificateController (OLD, basic)
- /admin/certificate-queue → CertificateQueueController (NEW, complete Phase 1-6)

SOLUTION:
Redirect /admin/certificates ke /admin/certificate-queue
